# Code of Conduct

Squirix welcomes participation regardless of background, experience, identity, or affiliation.
Everyone participating in the project is expected to help maintain a respectful and constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other participants with respect and assume good intent while taking concerns seriously.
- Discuss technical ideas with evidence and explain disagreements without personal attacks.
- Give constructive feedback and respect differing experience levels and perspectives.
- Respect privacy, personal boundaries, and requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Acknowledge mistakes and cooperate in resolving conflicts.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or encouragement of violence.
- Personal insults, derogatory comments, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Sexual harassment, unwanted sexual attention, or sexualized content directed at participants.
- Publishing private information without consent, including private correspondence and identifying information.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in its investigation.
- Repeated unwanted contact or attempts to evade moderation restrictions.

Good-faith criticism of software, designs, and project decisions is welcome. Disagreement alone is not misconduct.

## Scope

This policy applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, project-managed chat spaces, and events.
It also applies when someone explicitly represents Squirix in another community or public setting.

## Reporting concerns

Email [admin@squirix.io](mailto:admin@squirix.io) to report a concern privately.
Include a description, relevant links or evidence, and any immediate safety or privacy concerns.
Do not post sensitive reports in public issues.

Reports are handled by the [project maintainer](MAINTAINERS.md). Information will be shared only as needed to assess
the report and respond, or when disclosure is required by law.
No reporter is required to confront the person involved before making a report.

Anyone reviewing a report must disclose a conflict of interest and seek an uninvolved reviewer where one is available.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will consider the available evidence, context, severity, and any repeated behavior.
Responses may include a request to stop or correct behavior, removal of content, a warning, a temporary restriction,
or a permanent exclusion from project spaces. Serious incidents may require immediate restrictions.

When practical, the person affected by a moderation decision will receive an explanation and any conditions for returning.
They may request reconsideration by emailing the reporting address with additional context.
Moderation decisions and reports must not be used to intimidate participants or suppress good-faith technical discussion.
