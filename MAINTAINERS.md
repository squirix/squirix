## Contact

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/squirix/squirix/issues) for bugs, feature requests, and roadmap proposals.
For other project inquiries, email [admin@squirix.io](mailto:admin@squirix.io).

Report security vulnerabilities through the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Report community conduct concerns as described in [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors can take part through code, documentation, issue triage, and reviews; see [contributing.md](contributing.md).
Maintainer appointments consider sustained contributions, technical judgment, respectful collaboration, and willingness
to share responsibility for the project. Changes to this list are reviewed through pull requests.
